Doki Doki Debate Club

A visual novel that tells both a tragedy and heroic triumph of seemingly ordinary debate club members.
To play the game, you can left click, space, or scroll through the visuals.

Our team was formed by our friend group ROS which consists of Jesus Armendariz (Storyboard Writer), Emerick Hernandez (Music and Art), and Alan Valdivia (Programmer)
